The public build-1142 archive contains the identical WebClient WASM, but its encoded `InExperience.rbxm` differs in size from the missing owner patch; active flags and the owner's executed UI code are unknown. The original 1142 owner patch was absent. The newer 1145 download’s InExperience patch is hash-matched to the public 1145 patch, and its legacy BackpackScript module hash matches the candidate below. The user’s active experiment branch is still unknown.

The loose archive's `BackpackScript.lua` SHA256 is `7da5e99aa907d8b335a62eeae4cc2b8a22ec7f28ca35823f955e6702fdf6edcd`; the candidate compiled module's SHA256 is `bc01924136dcab1b502f32f83af708379465934f744dda8a746aea4be32ef087`. Against the same unmapped candidate compiled prototypes executed in official native Luau 0.745, tests matched 4 layout cases, 122 position cases, 16,512 visibility cases, 12 selection cases and 6 equip cases. These are mocked differential tests, not an end-to-end Roblox runtime comparison.

## Item and rendering

The item is the public Roblox Linked Sword Tool asset `125013769` (current response SHA256 `99d7e509a7d34d237e5d19aa4effa35b383c61dbc90fb91118364ee27692a68c`), with its public mesh `12221720` (`1c5eb33db31b3cd97c2e036df085276a8df7e6585ff735dfecfc92a9b0b3e768`), texture `12224218` (`0829b4fd05fd19e8cc57543e480d0f3e1e0e9fa6eb6ce97f08aee5ae6f228e88`), and icon `124987047` (`ad34c540a053f5856e3d23977c160a2fd6e5d8227d3ad0f2900dfdfd1b0ddcf9`). These public assets were fetched separately and are not asserted to have been supplied in the owner's folder or to be the historical bytes used by any particular experience. No ownership of the asset is implied.

The 198-triangle v1.00 mesh is converted to browser geometry by multiplying positions by 0.5 and flipping V. The rendered position composes the archived R6 `RightGripAttachment`, an Rx(-90°) R6 weld rotation, the inverse public Tool `Grip`, and the current candidate right-arm frame. The rotation follows community R6 weld examples, not a traced WASM engine routine. Materials, pose interpolation, weld, and rendering are browser adapters and have not been verified against the runtime.

The public SwordScript uses base/slash/lunge damage values 5/10/30, an activation interval of 0.2 seconds, and Slash/Lunge animation requests. Selected Attack/Lunge/Activated functions matched 8,192 cases against official Luau with mocked services. `linked-sword-hit.js` transcribes the public IsTeamMate/TagHumanoid/UntagHumanoid/Blow checks and matched 8,640 mocked Luau cases. That does not verify Roblox Touched events or the engine physics. The gray dummy, its 100 health and five-second respawn, swept segment/AABB contacts, creator-tag mock, and damage response are authored demo adapters, not a recovered Humanoid, place, multiplayer combat or exact hitbox. The public Handle is 1×0.8×4; the demo uses an approximate half-stud contact expansion. Damage at actual browser contact follows the adapted script state, so a normal swing may deliver base damage 5 after the source's immediate reset. Volume affects the three embedded public sword sounds. Roblox network, real targets, team services, weapon drops and replication are absent.

This is an input-adapter correction, not proof of the Roblox input implementation.

## Dummy death and overhead display

The default Humanoid API documents `BreakJointsOnDeath = true`, `HealthDisplayType = DisplayWhenDamaged`, and 100-stud name/health distances. The authored demo now separates six visible R6-shaped parts when the dummy dies and shows its name; the overhead health bar appears only after damage and changes green/yellow/red. The initial impulses, gravity integration, ground response, projection, text styling and color interpolation are browser approximations; inter-part collision, occlusion, engine physics, and exact Roblox overhead GUI rasterization are not reconstructed. The dummy respawns after an authored five seconds. References: [Humanoid API](https://robloxapi.github.io/ref/class/Humanoid.html) and [Roblox character name/health display](https://create.roblox.com/docs/characters/name-health-display).
